Snap your insurance card. Get a plain-English read on your plan.

Upload your health insurance card, and your benefits document if you have it. Claude pulls out the numbers that matter (copays, deductible, what's covered, what needs prior approval) and tells you what to do with them.

Ask about your plan

CareCard explains what's printed on your card and in the documents you upload. It isn't your insurer and can't confirm coverage, eligibility or what a specific provider will bill. Always verify with the member services number on your card before a big appointment or procedure.
